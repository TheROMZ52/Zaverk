## Žavërk — Language Guide

[English](README.en.md) · [فارسی](README.fa.md)

### A conlang with its own alphabet and grammar, because one more language on Earth apparently wasn't enough.

## 02 / GRAMMAR

### 1. Sentence order

The base structure is:

**Subject + Object + Verb**

Example:

```text
Në Vü Drøklæv.
```

"I love you."

### 2. Pronouns

| English | Žavërk |
|---|---|
| I | Në |
| You | Vü |
| He / She / It | Ën |
| We | Nëm |
| You (plural/formal) | Vën |
| They | Ënra |

### 3. The verb "to be"

`Bë` means "to be".

| English | Žavërk |
|---|---|
| I am | Bë |
| You are | Bë |
| He / She / It is | Bë |
| We are | Bën |
| You are (plural) | Bën |
| They are | Bën |

Example:

```text
Në Bë Mëla.
```

"I am good."

### 4. Past tense

The verb changes with a person-specific suffix to form the past tense.

Example verb: `Varn` — to go

| English | Žavërk |
|---|---|
| I went | Varnë |
| You went | Varnï |
| He / She / It went | Varnø |
| We went | Varnëm |
| You went (plural) | Varnët |
| They went | Varnën |

### 5. Present tense

| English | Žavërk |
|---|---|
| I go | Varna |
| You go | Varni |
| He / She / It goes | Varnor |
| We go | Varnem |
| They go | Varnen |

Example: `Në Varna.` — "I go."

### 6. Future tense

The suffix `-ul` is used to form the future.

`Varnul` means "will go".

Example:

```text
Në Varnul.
```

"I will go."

### 7. Negation

Use `Næ` to make a sentence negative.

```text
Næ Varna.
Næ Bë Mëla.
```

"I do not go." and "I am not good."

### 8. Pluralization

The suffix `-en` is generally used to form plurals.

`Drøk → Drøken`

"Friend → Friends"

### 9. Adjectives

Adjectives usually come after the noun.

- `Käv Mëla.` — good house
- `Şevar Grön.` — big city

### 10. Questions

For yes/no questions, keep the sentence structure and place a question mark at the end.

```text
Vü Bë Mëla?
```

"Are you good?"

- `Ära` — yes
- `Næ` — no

### 11. Possession

Use `de` to indicate possession.

- `Käv de Në.` — my house
- `VæƵ de Vü.` — your game

---

## 03 / CORE SENTENCE PATTERNS

**Verb sentence:**

Subject + Object + Verb

`Në Vü Drøklæv.` — I love you.

**Descriptive sentence:**

Subject + `Bë` + Adjective

`Në Bë Mëla.` — I am good.

---

## 04 / RULE SUMMARY

- Base sentence order: Subject + Object + Verb.
- Adjectives usually follow nouns.
- The usual plural suffix is `-en`.
- Use `Næ` for negation.
- Use `-ul` for the future tense.
- Verbs change across tenses.
- Special letters such as `Æ`, `Ø`, `Ë`, `Ƶ`, `Š`, `Ž`, and `Ð` have distinct pronunciations.
- Words are generally pronounced close to their written form.

> Žavërk and its rules are experimental and may change as the language develops.
